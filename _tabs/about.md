#### **Who are you?**  
Hi, I’m Cephas — a 21-year-old final-year Cyber Security student at De Montfort University.

#### **How would you describe yourself?**  
I’m a naturally curious person. I like to explore the "why" and "how" behind things, and I enjoy connecting different ideas in creative ways. I’m passionate about what I do and I care deeply about helping others grow and succeed.

#### **What are you into?**  
I find a lot of things interesting — space, technology, systems, and anything that mixes logic with creativity. My main focus is cyber security and software development, but I’ve recently been exploring mechatronics, and I’ve also dabbled in game development, which was pretty cool. I'm always looking to explore both the breadth *and* depth of these topics (and maybe a few unexpected ones too).

#### **How do you learn?**  
By doing — trial and error, breaking and fixing, and making things hands-on. I learn best when I’m immersed, sketching out ideas, testing things, and building connections between concepts. I like to engrain knowledge into how I see the world — constantly linking ideas and keeping an open perspective.

#### **What’s this site for?**  
This is my space to document what I’m working on, share ideas, and track my growth. Some parts are polished, others are mid-build — and that’s the point. It’s part blog, part portfolio, and part personal lab notebook.

#### **What do you enjoy outside of tech?**  
I love playing sports — especially football and basketball. I also like reading: whether it’s novels, my Bible, or the occasional self-help book. Most recently, I’ve been reading:  
> *How to Build a Second Brain* by Tiago Forte

#### **What’s next?**  
I’m not sure exactly where I’m heading yet — but I know I want to keep learning, stay useful, and build things that matter, even if it’s one small project at a time.
